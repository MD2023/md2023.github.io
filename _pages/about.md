Hello everyone! I am Michelle Dai, a 3rd year Ph.D student at Johns Hopkins University in the [Applied Math and Statistics Department](https://engineering.jhu.edu/ams/). I currently conduct research with [Dr. Yanxun Xu](https://www.ams.jhu.edu/~yxu70/) on using multi-objective reinforcement learning to determine ideal treatments for patients with HIV. For my undergraduate studies, I attended Princeton University, where I earned a B.S.E. in Operations Research and Financial Engineering and minors in Statistics & Machine Learning and Finance. I completed an [undergraduate thesis](https://dataspace.princeton.edu/handle/88435/dsp0112579w54m) under the guidance of [Dr. Jianqing Fan](https://fan.princeton.edu) on using feedforward neural networks to predict American options prices. Between my undergraduate and graduate studies, I worked at the [World Bank Treasury](https://treasury.worldbank.org/en/about/unit/treasury) in Washington, DC, having been inspired by its goals to eradicate poverty and promote shared prosperity worldwide.
